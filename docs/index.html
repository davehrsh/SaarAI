04 / THE NAME

Find the saar in every bite.

The name SaarAI draws from the Sanskrit word Saar (सार), meaning essence or the essential nature of something, combined with AI. It reflects our aim: to help uncover the essence of the food you eat, one image at a time.

05 / YOUR PRIVACY

Food insights should feel simple. Privacy should too.

SaarAI is designed without mandatory accounts or a permanent in-app food history. Images are sent to the backend for analysis, while third-party AI and infrastructure services may process content or technical information under their own policies.

Designed with restraint.

We aim to collect and retain only what is needed to deliver the service. Read the full policy for details about image processing, third-party services, retention and your privacy rights.
